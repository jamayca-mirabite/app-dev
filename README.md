
# My Favorite Movie

## Harry Potter

**Harry Potter** is my favorite movie series. It is about a young wizard named Harry Potter who studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

### Why I Like It
I like Harry Potter because it has magic, adventure, friendship, and exciting stories. It teaches us to be brave, kind, and loyal to our friends.

### My Favorite Characters
- Harry Potter
- Hermione Granger
- Ron Weasley
- Albus Dumbledore

### My Favorite Things About the Movie
1. Magic and spells
2. Friendship and teamwork
3. Amazing adventures
4. Interesting characters

### My Rating
**10/10 – Highly Recommended!**

*Harry Potter is a magical story that I will always enjoy watching.*
